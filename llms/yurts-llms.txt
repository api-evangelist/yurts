# Legion Intelligence

> Legion Intelligence builds governed agentic AI for national security. Legion deploys AI agents that carry out work across the systems teams already use: humans command, agents operate, and every action is attributed. Legion is delivered through Legion Packs, role-based capability bundles that put agents to work on day one, and extends to disconnected tactical environments through Centurion by Legion Intelligence. Legion runs in cloud, on-prem, air-gapped, classified (IL2 through IL6), and edge environments.

**Legion Intelligence** ("Legion" after first mention) turns human intent into coordinated, governed action. Humans define objectives, constraints, and priorities; AI agents carry out the work across systems and data; governance enforces policy, provides full attribution, and keeps humans in command at every step. Legion Intelligence is led by co-founder and CEO Ben Van Roo.

Legion is built for national security: the U.S. Department of War (DoW), the intelligence community, the U.S. Department of Energy, and public-sector-adjacent organizations. The same capability and governance extend to critical infrastructure and adjacent industries, including aerospace, manufacturing, maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO), energy, and pharmaceutical production.

## Centurion by Legion Intelligence

Centurion by Legion Intelligence is a deployable edge AI system integrating hardware, software, and sustainment for DDIL (denied, degraded, intermittent, limited connectivity) environments. Centurion runs the same Legion Packs and the same governance as cloud and on-prem deployments, from a single node to a coordinated mesh. Cloud connectivity is an accelerator when available, never a requirement for operation.

Model routing is automatic. When connected, Centurion routes to the latest customer-approved frontier models. When degraded or disconnected, it shifts to the latest locally hosted open-weight models with no operator intervention, and updated models deploy to edge nodes with controlled rollout, so the edge never becomes a frozen AI island.

Four deployment models (May 2026):

- **Centurion III** (1 user): Intel-based laptop built on Intel Core Ultra Series 3 processors, for forward operators and small teams.
- **Centurion V** (up to 2 users): NVIDIA DGX Spark, backpack-portable for small-team mobile deployment and forward operational bases.
- **Centurion X** (up to 50 users): HPE Edgeline EL8000 with NVIDIA GPU, for vehicle-mounted or forward operating base use, with rugged case options tested to MIL-STD-810G and MIL-STD-461 and TAA-compliant configurations available.
- **Centurion L** (up to 350 users): HPE ProLiant DL380a Gen12 with multi-NVIDIA GPU, for FOB datacenters, embassies, and air-gapped networks.

Procurement is a single purchase: hardware, software, models, and support in one line item, with fixed pricing, a known BOM, and HBOM/SBOM documentation ready for accreditation. Solution delivery and ecosystem partners include Intel, HPE, Rancher Government Solutions, Google, and NVIDIA.

## How Legion Works

Customers interact with four things: Packs, Spaces, Apps, and Collections. A **Pack** is a role-based bundle that deploys a ready working environment. A **Space** is a structured environment where teams organize and carry out work, defining who has access, what context is available, and how work is coordinated. An **App** is an AI-powered tool that completes a specific task from start to finish (examples: SITREP Generator, Award Writer, Manual Assistant, Work Order Scheduler). A **Collection** is the governed knowledge layer that powers every result with permissions and provenance.

Across every Pack, Legion agents search an organization's entire document ecosystem and trace every result back to source; answer questions in plain language with citations; draft emails, reports, and proposals grounded in organizational data, with human review before anything ships; and run no-code agentic workflows that carry multi-step work across systems end to end, with humans approving the steps that matter.

Underneath, Legion connects to 100+ systems (including IBM Maximo, SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, ServiceNow, Jira, and Box), supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), offers REST API extensibility, and runs any model: open-source, proprietary, or fine-tuned. New models are added through the Model hub without code changes.

## Security and Deployment

Legion is FedRAMP High authorized (cloud platform), NIST 800-53 compliant, SOC 2 Type II certified, and CMMC Level 2, and is ready to manage ITAR-controlled data. Deployment is authorized from IL2 through IL6, including air-gapped networks. HIPAA and GDPR attestations are in place, and HBOM/SBOM documentation supports accreditation.

Data sovereignty is enforced by design: customer data stays in the customer's environment and is never used to train models. Role-based and attribute-based access controls (RBAC and ABAC), approval workflows, guardrails, and audit logging govern every action, including agent actions, with full attribution showing what was done and on whose behalf.

Deployment options: cloud, on-prem, hybrid, air-gapped, and edge (through Centurion).

**Q: What is Legion Intelligence?**

**Q: What does DDIL mean?**
A: DDIL stands for denied, degraded, intermittent, and limited connectivity. DDIL describes operating environments where network access cannot be assumed. Centurion by Legion Intelligence is built for DDIL conditions: it runs Legion Packs on locally hosted models with no cloud dependency and synchronizes when connectivity returns and policy permits.

**Q: How does Legion Intelligence keep humans in command?**
